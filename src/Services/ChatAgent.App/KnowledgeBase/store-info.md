## Ordering, Pickup and Delivery

### Pickup

- Pickup orders are usually ready in 15–20 minutes.
- Choose your pickup time at checkout; the earliest slot is 20 minutes from ordering.
- Collect at the counter to the left of the main entrance and quote your order number.
- Orders are held for 45 minutes past the chosen slot, after which they may be discarded
  and no refund is given.

### Delivery

- **Delivery radius:** 8 km from the restaurant.
- **Delivery fee:** 25,000 VND flat, free for orders over 400,000 VND.
- **Typical delivery time:** 30–45 minutes, longer at peak (11:30–13:00 and 18:00–20:00).
- **Minimum order for delivery:** 120,000 VND.
- Delivery is handled by our own riders; you will receive an SMS when the rider departs.

### Reservations

- Tables can be reserved for groups of 4 or more, up to 30 days ahead.
- Reservations are held for 15 minutes past the booked time.
- Groups of 10 or more require a deposit of 200,000 VND, deducted from the final bill.

## Payment

- Accepted: cash, Visa, Mastercard, JCB, MoMo, ZaloPay and VNPay.
- We do not accept cheques, cryptocurrency, or foreign currency.
- Prices on the menu include VAT. No service charge is added for parties under 10.
- A 5% service charge applies to parties of 10 or more.
- Electronic invoices (hoa don dien tu) are issued on request within 7 days of the order.

## Refunds and Cancellations

### Cancelling an order

- Orders can be cancelled free of charge until the kitchen starts preparing them, which is
  normally within 5 minutes of ordering.
- After preparation starts, cancellation is not possible for freshly cooked items.
- To cancel, use the order screen in the app or call +84 28 7300 1234.

### Refunds

- If an item arrives wrong, missing, cold, or below our standard, we will replace it or
  refund it in full.
- Report a problem within **2 hours** of delivery or pickup, with a photo where possible.
- Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**.
- Cash orders are refunded as store credit or cash at the counter, whichever you prefer.
- We do not refund orders simply because a customer changed their mind after the food has
  been prepared.

### Late deliveries

- If a delivery arrives more than 30 minutes past the quoted window, the delivery fee is
  refunded automatically.
- If it arrives more than 60 minutes late, we also add a 50,000 VND credit to your account.

## Allergens and Dietary Information

- Common allergens across our menu include peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish, fish sauce, soy,
  sesame, egg, dairy and gluten.
- Our kitchen is **not** allergen-free. Cross-contact is possible for every dish, so we
  cannot guarantee any item is completely free of a given allergen.
- Vegetarian and vegan dishes are marked on the menu. Vegan dishes are prepared without
  fish sauce, shrimp paste, egg or dairy.
- Tell us about an allergy in the order notes or by phone before ordering, and speak to
  staff directly for severe allergies.
- Spice level can be adjusted on most dishes: mild, medium or hot.

## Loyalty, Coupons and Promotions

- **Mango Points:** earn 1 point per 10,000 VND spent; 100 points converts to a
  50,000 VND discount.
- Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
- Coupon codes are entered at checkout. One coupon per order — coupons do not stack, and
  they cannot be combined with Mango Points on the same order.
- Coupons apply to food and drink only, never to the delivery fee.
- **Happy hour:** 15% off drinks Monday to Thursday, 14:00–17:00, dine-in only.
- **Student discount:** 10% off dine-in with a valid student card, excluding set menus.
- Expired or invalid codes are rejected at checkout; we cannot apply a coupon after an
  order has been placed.

## Group Bookings and Catering

- Catering is available for 20–200 guests with 72 hours' notice.
- Set menus start at 250,000 VND per person; a custom menu can be arranged.
- A 30% deposit confirms a catering booking and is non-refundable within 48 hours of the event.
- Equipment, serving staff and delivery outside the 8 km radius are quoted separately.

## Privacy and Data

- We store your name, phone number, email and delivery address to fulfil orders.
- Payment card details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored by us.
- Chat conversations with Mango AI are stored so you can see your history and so we can
  improve the service.
- To request a copy of your data or have it deleted, email
  support@mango-restaurant.example from your registered address.
- We do not sell customer data to third parties.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you take walk-ins?**
Yes. Reservations are only needed for groups of 4 or more.

**Is there Wi-Fi?**
Yes, free guest Wi-Fi. The network is `Mango-Guest` and the password is printed on the
table card.

**Are pets allowed?**
Assistance animals are welcome everywhere. Other pets are welcome on the outdoor terrace only.

**Do you have high chairs or a kids' menu?**
Yes to both. High chairs are available on request and the kids' menu is on the last page.

**Can I change an order after placing it?**
Only before the kitchen starts preparing it. Call us as soon as possible.

**Do you cater to large office lunches?**
Yes — see the catering section above, or email hello@mango-restaurant.example.

**Where can I leave feedback?**
Email support@mango-restaurant.example, or use the feedback link in your order receipt.
